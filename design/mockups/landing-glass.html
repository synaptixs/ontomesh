What you can build

Three things you'd otherwise hand-craft for months.

Generate a full ontology from a schema.

Point Ontomesh at a database. It introspects the schema, lifts it through OWL 2 / SHACL / JSON-LD, runs the reasoner, and emits a bundle with full lineage.

316 MB DB · 4.2 s · 142 classes, 1.1k properties

Validate with SHACL. Reason with Datalog.

Shapes catch data-quality issues before production. Datalog rules derive new facts with full provenance. Every run emits a diffable materialisation report.

38 SHACL shapes · SWRL · Datalog (Rulewerk)

Mine candidates from raw logs.

Log Discovery mines templates with online clustering, surfaces candidate entities and events, and gates causal edges — you review one candidate at a time.

L4 templates · L8 regimes · L11 causal DAG

How it works

From raw signal to graph-grounded RAG, in three steps.

1

Discover

Point Ontomesh at logs or a schema. Candidates surface with confidence, provenance, and a one-click accept.

2

Model & validate

Add SHACL shapes and Datalog rules. The reasoner materialises derived triples; the CQ runner gives you regression coverage.

3

Generate & ship

One click emits OWL Turtle, SHACL, JSON-LD, and bindings — plus a hybrid retriever ready for your RAG stack.
